Questions

What devices do we need?
Any recent tablet, phone or laptop with a web browser: iPad, Android, Chromebook, Windows or Mac. Nothing to install. Judges usually use tablets; the check-in desk and stage crew can use whatever's to hand.
Do we need internet at the venue?
Not all the time. Tablets keep working offline and catch up when they reconnect. With the Season plan you can also run Spotlight on a laptop on your own network, with no internet at all, and everything still syncs between devices.
Do judges need their own accounts?
Yes. Each judge gets a username, so their scores are theirs alone and nobody else can change them. You create the accounts in a minute, and several people can share a tablet with “Switch person”.
Can contestants see their scores?
Only if you choose. You can give each contestant their average score, category averages, the judges' quick notes and comments. It never shows which judge gave what.
Who owns the data?
You do. Export results as a spreadsheet or download a full backup at any time. We back everything up automatically, and never share or sell your contestants' details.
Can we try it first?
Yes. Ask for a demo organization in the form below and run a practice contest with your team before you commit.
