    How a show runs

    One app for everyone working the contest. Each person signs in and sees only their part of it.

    1. Open sign-ups

      Share a link or QR code. Entries land in your contest, waiting for your approval.

    2. Check people in

      The desk checks entrants in, places walk-ups in heats, and fixes details on the spot.

    3. Run the stage

      Your crew sees who's on and who's next. A monitor backstage shows the emcee the same.

    4. Judge

      Each judge scores alone, on their own tablet, with quick notes and their own running ranking.

    5. Announce and follow up

      Standings, finals and the podium are ready at once. Contestants get their feedback.

    Every judge scores on their own

    Judges can't see each other's scores, so nobody anchors on the loudest voice at the table. Each judge sees their own running ranking, with gold, silver and bronze for their current top three.

    • Your criteria, maximums and weights
    • Quick notes, like “extraordinary paint”, in one tap
    • Scores save on the tablet first, so nothing is lost if the Wi-Fi drops

    Heats and finals, without the spreadsheet

    Split a big contest into heats with a set number of places, and keep walk-up places free. Spotlight finds the finalists across all heats, then scores the finals from zero.

    • Places and walk-up slots per heat
    • Finalists picked from all heats combined
    • Ties are flagged for you to decide, never broken silently

    From sign-up link to stage call

    Contestants sign up on their phones with your own questions and reference photos. You approve them, the check-in desk confirms who's here, and the running order is ready for the stage.

    • A link and QR code for each contest
    • Contestants can withdraw with their own private link
    • A backstage monitor for the emcee, no sign-in needed

    Contestants leave with more than a placing

    Every contestant can get their average score, their average for each category, the notes the judges ticked and their written comments. It never says which judge gave what.

    • Print a sheet for everyone, or one at a time
    • Email each contestant their own feedback
    • Or show it on their private sign-up page

    Keeps working when the Wi‑Fi doesn't

    Convention Wi-Fi fails at the worst moment. Spotlight is built for it.

    Every tablet keeps working offline and catches up when it reconnects. For venues with no internet at all, run Spotlight on a laptop on your own network: everything still syncs between devices, including the stage timer. And if a tablet dies, print paper score cards in a few seconds.
