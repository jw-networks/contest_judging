How Spotlight is organized

Spotlight has three levels:

  • Your organization: your convention, club or group. It has its own accounts and data, separate from everyone else's.
  • Events: a show on a date, like “Spring Con 2027”.
  • Contests: the competitions within an event, like “Cosplay masquerade” or “Prop makers”. Each contest has its own criteria, entries, judges and results.

Contests are either cosplay (with a stage, running order, tech booth and emcee) or makers (judged entries, no stage).

Everyone uses the same app at spotlight.drimworksprops.com/app. What each person sees depends on their role in the contest they're working on.

Signing in

Open the app, enter your organization code, your username and your password. The easiest way is your organization's sign-in link, which fills in the code for you.

  • First sign-in: you'll be asked to choose your own password.
  • Forgot your password? Use the link on the sign-in screen. If your account has an email address, you'll get a link to choose a new one. Otherwise, ask an admin to reset it.
  • Sharing a tablet: use Switch person in the account menu. Each person keeps their own sign-in and scores.
  • Install it: add the app to the home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen on iPad, or the install option in Chrome). It opens full screen and works offline.

Roles

Admins manage the whole organization. Everyone else gets a role in each contest they work on, and can have different roles in different contests.

AdminEverything: events, contests, accounts, backups. Admins see every contest.
LeadRuns a contest: setup, criteria, sign-ups, results, score review, feedback and the audit log.
CoordinatorCheck-in and the stage: confirms who's here, runs the order, calls people on stage. Can't see scores.
JudgeScores entries. Sees only their own scores and their own ranking.
TechThe tech booth: who's on stage and their music and lighting cues.
EmceeWho's on stage and next, with pronunciation and intro notes.
ViewerRead-only results and check-in, for organizers who just want to watch.

Setting up a contest

Admins create events and contests on the Admin page. Then, in each contest's Setup:

  1. Criteria: what judges score, each with a maximum and an optional weight. The total is each score times its weight.
  2. Quick notes: the tick boxes judges can use, like “extraordinary paint” or “great weathering”. They're used for contestant feedback later.
  3. Divisions (or categories, for makers), if you split entries by skill level or type.
  4. People: give accounts a role in this contest. Admins create accounts on the Admin page.
  5. Entries: add them one at a time, open sign-ups, or paste rows from a spreadsheet or choose a CSV file. Columns can include number, name, character, series, division, heat, contact details and stage notes.

Do a short practice run with your judges before the show: score two or three test entries so everyone knows the screen.

Sign-up links

In Setup → Sign-ups, turn sign-ups on to get a link and a QR code for the contest. Download the QR code as an image for posters and tables.

  • Review: by default, sign-ups wait as pending review in Check-in until someone approves, waitlists or declines them. Turn review off to accept everyone straight away.
  • Waitlist: tap Waitlist to hold someone's place in line without approving them yet. Waitlisted people don't take a place, so they don't count toward a full contest or a full heat. They see their number in line on their private page. Approve them when a place opens: with heats on, Spotlight puts them in a heat with a free place.
  • Your questions: name, character and email are required by default. Each built-in question can be required, optional or off. You can also add your own questions, and contestants can attach up to three reference photos.
  • Limits: set a closing time or a maximum. With heats, sign-ups only fill regular places, so walk-up places stay free.
  • Withdrawing: each contestant gets a private link to their entry, and can withdraw until they go on stage.
  • New link: stops the old link and QR code working straight away.

With email set up, contestants get a confirmation with their private link, then an email when you approve, waitlist or decline them.

Heats and finals

Turn on heats in Setup for big contests. Each heat has a number of regular places plus walk-up places (14 and 4 by default).

  • Entries can be placed in heats on import (a heat column), at sign-up, or at check-in.
  • Judges pick the heat they're scoring. Each judge has an All heats tab to look back at earlier rounds.
  • Finalists are the top entries across all heats combined (three by default), not the top of each heat.
  • Finals are scored from zero. The podium comes from the finals scores.

Check-in

The Check-in screen lists every entry, with search by name, character, number or email.

  • Tap an entry to check them in, or to edit their details.
  • Pending online sign-ups show at the top with Approve, Waitlist and Decline. The Waitlist filter lists waitlisted people in order.
  • Add walk-ups on the spot. With heats on, Spotlight shows how many places are left in each heat.
  • Mark people who don't turn up as no-shows. They're left out of results.

Running the stage

On the Stage screen, the coordinator sees the running order and calls each entry on stage. Everyone else's screens follow along live.

  • The Tech booth sees who's on and their music and lighting cues.
  • The Emcee sees who's on and who's next, with pronunciation and intro notes.
  • Tech and emcee screens follow the live stage, and they can also move on by themselves at any time: Next contestant, Back, or tap any name in the running order. That only changes their own screen, never the real stage. As soon as the main stage moves to someone else, their screen follows it again, or they can tap Follow live stage. This also keeps them going if their device loses its connection.
  • The confidence monitor is a full-screen view for a screen backstage or by the stage. Open it from the monitor button in the top bar. It needs no sign-in.

Judging

Judges see the Scoring screen with the entry on stage, or they can pick any entry in the running order.

  • Score each criterion, tick any quick notes, and add a comment if you like.
  • Scores save as you go. They're a draft until you tap Submit. Only submitted scores count in results.
  • Your running ranking shows gold, silver and bronze for your current top three. It's yours alone: judges never see each other's scores.

Leads and admins can check every judge's sheet on the Review page, and correct a score if needed. Every change is in the audit log.

Paper score cards

A backup for when tablets can't be used. Print them from Setup → Judging criteria or from Results.

  • Cards: one per entry, two per page, with a box for each criterion, a total, quick-note tick boxes and comment lines.
  • Judge sheet: one table per judge, a row per entry in running order.
  • Print for everyone, a heat, the finals, or blank cards only. Add blank cards for walk-ups, and a named copy for each judge.

Afterwards, type the scores in on each entry's Review page.

Offline and local mode

When the Wi-Fi drops

Every device keeps working. Scores, check-ins and stage changes are saved on the device and sent as soon as it's back online. The status in the top bar shows whether a device is online and synced.

Before switching off or changing devices, make sure the status shows everything is synced.

No internet at all

Run Spotlight on a laptop on your own network (a travel router works well). Download it from the Admin page: it runs on Windows, Mac, Linux or a Raspberry Pi, with nothing else to install. Devices connect to the laptop's address, and everything syncs between them, including the stage, without internet.

To move contests between the online app and a local server, use Transfer contests on the Admin page: export a file from one, import it into the other, then bring the results back after the show.

Results

The Results page ranks entries by their average: the mean of each judge's weighted total. It updates as scores are submitted.

  • Ties are flagged for you to decide. Spotlight never breaks a tie silently.
  • With heats, finalists are marked, and the finals have their own standings and podium.
  • Print the results, or export them as a spreadsheet (CSV).

Contestant feedback

In Results → Contestant feedback, leads and admins can share each contestant's own feedback:

  • their average score and their average for each category
  • the quick notes the judges ticked, most-ticked first
  • the judges' written comments, if you allow them

It never says which judge gave what. Print a sheet for everyone (or one from an entry's Review page), email each contestant their own, or show it on their private sign-up page.

Accounts and passwords

  • Admins create accounts on the Admin page with a temporary password, which the person changes at first sign-in.
  • Add an email to an account, or let people add their own in the account menu, so they can reset a forgotten password themselves.
  • Admins can reset a password or turn an account off at any time. Turning an account off signs that person out everywhere.

Backups and your data

  • Spotlight backs up your organization automatically, photos included, within about a minute of any change. Earlier versions are kept.
  • Admins can download a full backup from the Admin page at any time, and export results as spreadsheets.
  • The Audit log records who changed what and when: scores, entries, check-ins and settings.
  • Contact details are only shown to leads, coordinators and admins. Judges, tech and emcees never see contestants' emails or phone numbers.

Need a backup restored? Contact support.
